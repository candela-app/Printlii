What are we building?

A system that lets a customer upload a document, pay, and walk away. They come back only when the printed pages are ready. The shop gets an organized queue instead of messages on WhatsApp.

The one idea to remember: the platform is a bridge between digital customer orders and the physical work of a print shop.

How to read this page
  1. Read the story and meet the people below.
  2. Open any diagram. Each one has two boxes: Process (what happens) and Situation (the same thing as a real day in the shop).
  3. Items marked FUTURE are not in the first version. TBD means not decided yet.

The story: a day at Hemadri Xerox

Hemadri Xerox is a busy print shop near a college. Every afternoon the queue reaches the door. Customers shout their names, send files on WhatsApp, and ask "Is it ready?" again and again. Hemadri decides to try a platform that fixes this.

PersonRoleWhat they do
HemadriOwnerOwns the shop, printers and money. Sets prices and rules.
ChandramohanManagerApproves big orders, handles refunds, watches the queue.
SaiOperatorLooks after Printer A (black and white, fast).
SatwikOperatorLooks after Printer B (color and A3). Checks printed pages.
AnilPickup deskChecks the pickup code and hands over papers.
ChaitanyaCashierCreates orders for walk-in customers who pay cash.
Rahul, Sandeep UI, NagaTejaCustomersStudents who need prints. NagaTeja orders from his hostel.

Their day, in ten steps

  1. Morning set-up. Hemadri opens the Print Station page on the shop PC and enters a one-time code. He adds Printer A and Printer B and tells the system what each can do.
  2. Rahul arrives. He scans a QR code, uploads his assignment, names it "College Assignment", and picks his options.
  3. Price and time. He sees ₹24 and "about 8 to 10 minutes", then pays by UPI.
  4. Approval. Small jobs print automatically. A very large one waits for Chandramohan.
  5. Choosing a printer. The system picks the best printer that can do the job.
  6. Printing. The job reaches the shop PC and prints on Sai's printer.
  7. Checking. Satwik looks at the pages. Only then does the order become "Ready".
  8. Notification. Rahul gets a message with a pickup code.
  9. Pickup. He shows the code. Anil verifies it and hands over the papers.
  10. Closing. Hemadri checks the day's sales, and the platform team checks that every shop ran smoothly.

Simple words used here

Merchant / ShopMerchant is the business owner (Hemadri). Shop is one location. One merchant can have many shops.
ResourceAny machine that does work, for now a printer.
Print StationA web page open on the shop PC. It receives jobs from the cloud and sends them to the printer. Nothing to install.
CloudThe online part that decides what to print and where.
ETAEstimated time until the order is ready. Shown as a range, because it is an estimate.
PriorityA paid option for a faster slot. Offered only when it really saves time.
Physical verificationA person checks the printed pages before the order is called Ready.
OTP / QRA short code or picture the customer shows at pickup.
WebhookA message the payment app sends to us when money arrives.
IdempotencyDoing the same request twice has the same result as doing it once. No double orders.
ReconciliationA night-time check that payments and orders match.
Audit logA diary of who did what and when.

3Order lifecycle state machine

Process

Payment, approval and printing are separate states. The ERROR path always goes through physical verification.

Swipe sideways to see the whole diagram.

stateDiagram-v2
[*] --> CREATED
CREATED --> UPLOADED
UPLOADED --> CONFIGURED
CONFIGURED --> PAYMENT_PENDING
PAYMENT_PENDING --> PAID
PAID --> AWAITING_APPROVAL: manual or rule-triggered
PAID --> APPROVED: auto print
AWAITING_APPROVAL --> APPROVED
AWAITING_APPROVAL --> CANCELLED: rejected, refund
APPROVED --> QUEUED
QUEUED --> RESOURCE_ASSIGNED
RESOURCE_ASSIGNED --> PRINTING
PRINTING --> PRINT_COMPLETED
PRINTING --> ERROR
ERROR --> PHYSICAL_VERIFICATION
PRINT_COMPLETED --> PHYSICAL_VERIFICATION
PHYSICAL_VERIFICATION --> READY_FOR_PICKUP
PHYSICAL_VERIFICATION --> QUEUED: retry or reassign
PHYSICAL_VERIFICATION --> CANCELLED: cancel, refund
READY_FOR_PICKUP --> COLLECTED
COLLECTED --> [*]

Situation

Sandeep UI's color order is PAID but waits for Chandramohan's approval. Then it prints, then Satwik checks the pages, and only then it becomes READY. Each step is a separate stage, so nothing jumps ahead.

4Payment flow with idempotent webhooks

Process

Gateways retry callbacks. The same event must never create two payments or orders.

Swipe sideways to see the whole diagram.

sequenceDiagram
participant Cu as Customer
participant API as Platform API
participant GW as Payment Gateway
participant DB as PostgreSQL
Cu->>API: Pay for order
API->>DB: Create payment PENDING with idempotency key
API->>GW: Create payment
GW-->>Cu: UPI payment screen
GW->>API: Webhook payment success
API->>DB: Event already processed?
alt Duplicate
 API-->>GW: 200 OK, ignore
else New
 API->>DB: Mark PAID, move order to approval flow
 API-->>GW: 200 OK
end
Note over API,DB: A reconciliation job compares gateway records with the DB and fixes paid-but-no-order cases

Situation

Rahul pays by UPI. His payment app sends the 'success' message twice. The platform spots the second copy and ignores it. He is charged once and gets one order.

31Payment reconciliation

Process

A scheduled job finds mismatches between the gateway and our database, so no customer is charged without an order and nobody is charged twice.

Swipe sideways to see the whole diagram.

flowchart TD
J["Reconciliation job, runs on a schedule"] --> G["Fetch gateway transactions"]
J --> D["Fetch platform payments and orders"]
G --> CMP{"Compare"}
D --> CMP
CMP -->|"Match"| OK["Nothing to do"]
CMP -->|"Paid at gateway, no order or still pending"| FIX["Recover: mark PAID, continue order"]
CMP -->|"Duplicate payment"| DUP["Refund or adjust the extra one"]
CMP -->|"Order paid, gateway shows none"| INV["Flag for manual investigation"]
FIX --> AUD[("Audit log")]
DUP --> AUD
INV --> AL["Alert Golden Admin"]

Situation

At night, Chandramohan's report shows NagaTeja paid but has no order. The platform finds his payment at the gateway, restores the order and tells him. He is never left charged with nothing.

53File delivery and prefetch (keeping it fast)

Process

Only small messages travel through the queue. The file moves once, straight from storage, and is prefetched so the printer never waits on the network. The original file is never modified.

Swipe sideways to see the whole diagram.

flowchart TD
U["Customer uploads direct to object storage with a presigned URL"] --> N["Server processes once: validate, page count, print-ready PDF copy, original untouched"]
N --> ST[("Encrypted object storage, India region")]
AP["Job APPROVED and queued"] --> MSG["Small job message to the Print Station"]
MSG --> LA{"In the next 1 to 2 jobs for its printer?"}
LA -->|"No"| WT["Wait: message only, no file yet"]
LA -->|"Yes"| PF["Prefetch via short-lived signed URL, resumable, checksum"]
ST --> PF
PF --> LC[("Local cache on shop PC")]
LC --> PR["Print when the printer is free"]
PR --> DEL["Delete local copy after printing"]
NET["Internet drops"] -.-> OFFL["Cached jobs keep printing, status syncs later with the same job id, no reprint"]
OFFL -.-> LC

Situation

Rahul's 40-page file is sent once. While Sai's printer finishes the earlier job, the shop PC already downloads his file. When the printer is free, it prints at once with no waiting on slow internet.
